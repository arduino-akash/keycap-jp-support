Keycap Jp — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Keycap Jp is a custom keyboard and translation app for iOS. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

Text you translate

When you translate text, the text you type is sent directly to DeepL, a third-party translation service, in order to return a translation. Keycap Jp does not operate its own servers and does not store your translated text outside of your device. DeepL's handling of this text is governed by their own privacy policy, linked above.

Data stored on your device

Translation history and pinned phrases are saved locally on your device, in a shared App Group container used by the app, its keyboard extension, and its share extension. This lets all three access the same history. This data is never uploaded to any server controlled by Keycap Jp, and is not shared with any third party other than DeepL (only for the text being actively translated, as described above).

You can clear your translation history at any time from within the app.

API keys

If you choose to add your own DeepL API key in Settings, that key is stored locally in the same shared App Group container and is used only to authenticate your own translation requests to DeepL.

What we don't do

  • We don't use analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't collect your name, email, contacts, or location.
  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
